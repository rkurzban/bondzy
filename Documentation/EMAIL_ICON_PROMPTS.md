# Bondzy Email Icon Prompts

Create these as a matched set of square PNG icons. Use 160 x 160 px. Use a clean flat vector-style email icon tile for Bondzy: dark navy rounded-square background (#1B2A4A), centered simple symbol in warm gold (#D4A843) and white, modern friendly polished app style, high contrast, no text, no letters, no watermark, no heavy 3D shadows, no photorealism, no gradients, no extra objects. Each icon must remain clear when displayed at 40 x 40 px in an email.

recipient.png - A friendly recipient/person symbol, simple bust or user silhouette, centered. Use white for the face/body shape with a small warm gold accent. No gendered details, no facial features, no text.

location.png - A clean location pin/map marker, centered. Use warm gold for the pin and a small white center circle. Make it friendly and rounded, not black, not realistic, not a Google Maps pin.

calendar.png - A simple calendar page, centered. White calendar body with warm gold top binding or date marks. No numbers, no text.

clock.png - A simple alarm clock or clock face, centered. White clock face with warm gold bells or hands. No numbers, no text.

reward.png - A wrapped gift box, centered. Warm gold box with white ribbon, friendly and simple. No confetti, no text.

promise.png - A promise or commitment symbol, centered. Use a simple handshake, linked hands, or interlocking loops. Warm gold and white only. Friendly, minimal, not detailed.

success.png - A successful checkmark inside a circle, centered. White circle or checkmark with warm gold accent. Clear at small size, no text.
